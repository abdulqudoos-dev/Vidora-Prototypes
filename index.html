Inside every prototype

The whole flowWelcome → Home → Create Your Identity → Image → Video → Editing Studio → Give It A Voice → Export and share. Each result links to the next step.
A dock you can customizeLong-press the mobile dock (or tap the grid icon) to add, remove and drag-reorder up to five tools. Your choice is remembered.
A CapCut-style studioMulti-track timeline, drag to scrub, split (S), duplicate, delete, trim handles, zoom, titles, auto-captions, filters, colour and aspect ratio.
Real uploadsDrop your own photos into Create Your Identity or the video start frame, or use the built-in sample set.
Believable AI statesLive progress with plain-English steps, skeleton loaders, results that animate in, and credits that go down as you generate.
⌘K command menuJump to any tool or action from the keyboard on web. Space plays the timeline in the studio.
Try this 60-second path in any prototype
  1. Get started → open Create Your Identity → “Use sample photos” → Create
  2. Go to Image → Generate → on any picture press Animate
  3. Generate the video → Open in Studio: play, press S to split, drag a trim handle
  4. Open Voice → Generate → Add to timeline → Export
Prototype images were generated with AI for this exploration. All interactions are simulated; no real generation or upload leaves the browser.
